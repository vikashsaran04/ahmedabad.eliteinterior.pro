Turnkey Execution of a Modern Minimalist 3BHK in South Bopal

A full architectural breakdown of client requirements, structural layout challenges, material specifications, and verified 58-day execution journey.

Section A

Project Overview & Key Parameters

Project: Turnkey Residential Interior

Locality: Gala Gymkhana Road, South Bopal, Ahmedabad

Property Type: 3BHK High-Rise Apartment

Carpet Area: 1,480 sq.ft.

Project Status: Successfully Handed Over

Turnaround Time: 58 Working Days

Figure 1: Handover view of the living and dining area with ambient 3000K illumination and fluted TV wall panelling.

Section B

The Client's Original Requirements

The homeowner, an IT professional with a spouse and two school-going children, had recently taken possession of a 3BHK apartment in South Bopal.

Their primary requirements included:

  • Clutter-Free Minimalism: A clean aesthetic inspired by Scandinavian functionality, avoiding excessive ornate mouldings or dark heavy veneers.
  • High-Storage Capacity: Floor-to-ceiling wardrobes with dedicated suitcase lofts, children's book storage, and an organized kitchen pantry.
  • Moisture & Heat Resistance in Kitchen: High-durability materials capable of enduring daily Gujarati cuisine and heavy spice usage.
  • Strict Fixed Budget: Protection against the common industry problem of unexpected 30% cost overruns mid-project.
Section C

Initial Challenges & Site Constraints

During the initial site laser audit conducted by Co-Founder Ramesh Taak, three distinct structural challenges were uncovered:

1. Deep RCC Beam Drop

An unplastered 14-inch concrete beam traversed the dining area, threatening to create an asymmetric ceiling line if a standard flat false ceiling was installed.

2. Narrow Kitchen Width

The builder's kitchen layout left only 7.5 feet of width, making an island impossible and requiring millimeter-precise clearance for refrigerator and dishwasher openings.

3. Society Work Hours

The apartment complex enforced a strict 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM noise window, requiring off-site factory modular manufacturing rather than noisy on-site cutting.

Section D

Design Strategy & Space Planning

Our design team formulated a synchronized plan addressing each structural constraint:

Cove Beam Integration: Instead of hiding the 14-inch beam behind an overly dropped ceiling that would diminish room height, we enclosed the beam in an architectural stepped soffit with indirect LED cove illumination, converting an awkward structural obstruction into an intentional spatial divider.

Parallel Modular Kitchen Layout: We engineered a high-efficiency parallel layout. The cooking zone (hob, spice pull-out, chimney) was positioned along the exterior ventilated wall, while tall pantry units and built-in appliances were aligned opposite to maintain a clean 42-inch central walkway.

Section F

The 8-Stage Execution Journey (58 Days)

DAYS 01–05

Site Prep & Conduiting

Flooring masking with protective corrugated sheets, laser grid marking, and wall chasing for electrical switchboard repositioning.

DAYS 06–15

Ceiling Framing & 2D Cutouts

Suspension of GI channels, installation of gypsum plasterboard, and cutouts for spotlights and AC drains.

DAYS 16–30

Factory Manufacturing

Precision CNC cutting of HDHMR carcasses, automated 2mm edge-banding with PUR water-resistant glue at our partner facility.

DAYS 31–45

Modular Assembly

On-site delivery of flat-pack modules, carcass levelling, countertop quartz template fitting, and wardrobe sliding track mounting.

DAYS 46–54

Painting & Wall Finishes

Three coats of acrylic putty sanding, primer seal, and double coats of Asian Paints Royale luxury emulsion.

DAYS 55–58

Snag Audit & Handover

Ramesh Taak completed our 50-point quality audit, checking all hinge tolerances, deep floor scrubbing, and handing over warranty booklets.
