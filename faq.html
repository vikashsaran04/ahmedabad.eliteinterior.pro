Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about planning, pricing, materials, payment milestones, and execution timelines for your Ahmedabad home interior.

Category 01

Hiring & Turnkey Scope

Hiring separate trades creates coordination chaos where the carpenter blames the electrician, and the painter blames the plasterer for imperfections. With Elite Interior, you deal with a single accountable partner who synchronizes civil, modular, false ceiling, and electrical works under one contract with guaranteed deadlines.
Our Co-Founder Ramesh Taak personally oversees site operations, material deliveries, and master carpentry alignments across Ahmedabad. You also have a designated site supervisor for daily coordination and a dedicated WhatsApp photo log managed by Vikash Kumar.

Category 02

Costs & Payment Milestones

Our payment structure is milestone-linked and transparent:
  • 10% Advance: Upon booking & design development commencement.
  • 40% Procurement: Upon final 2D/3D approval and material factory ordering.
  • 30% Site Delivery: Upon delivery of flat-pack modular carcasses to site.
  • 15% Installation: Upon completion of modular assembly and primer painting.
  • 5% Handover: Upon final 50-point snag clearance and key handoff.

The only reason a quote changes is if the homeowner explicitly requests additional furniture pieces, upgrades finishes (e.g., laminate to veneer), or alters civil walls after execution has begun. All changes are estimated and approved in writing first.
In Ahmedabad, a typical 2BHK flat turnkey interior ranges between ₹4.5 Lakh and ₹9.0 Lakh depending on finish grades, while a 3BHK ranges between ₹7.0 Lakh and ₹14.0+ Lakh. Explore our dedicated Interior Cost Guide 2026 for full line-item details.
Category 03

Materials, Quality & Warranties

For modular kitchens and bathroom vanities, we recommend Action TESA HDHMR or Calibrated Boiling Water Resistant (IS:710) Marine Plywood. For bedroom wardrobes and TV units, Calibrated Moisture Resistant (IS:303) Commercial Plywood or HDHMR with branded laminates (Century, Greenlam, Merino) ensures long-lasting stability in Ahmedabad's climate.
We pass direct manufacturer warranty cards to you for Hafele, Hettich, Ebco, and Blum hardware (typically covering 5 to 10 years depending on the brand model). In addition, Elite Interior provides 1 year of complimentary site alignment support for minor hinge adjustments.
Homeowners are welcome to visit their site at any time. We also schedule designated milestone walkthroughs: 1. After electrical conduiting, 2. After false ceiling framing, 3. After modular carcass assembly, and 4. Final pre-handover snag audit.
Category 04

Timelines & Handover

Typically, 2BHK flat interiors require 45 to 60 working days, and 3BHK flat interiors require 50 to 75 working days from final drawing approval. Bungalows and luxury penthouses generally require 75 to 120 working days depending on custom architectural detailing.
