Contract Checklist

What's Included vs. What's Often Excluded

Before signing an interior agreement with any firm, verify these specific line items:

Standard Elite Interior Turnkey Inclusions:
  • 2D furniture layouts & 3D photorealistic visualization
  • Calibrated core ply / HDHMR boards with branded laminates
  • Soft-closing hinges, tandem boxes, and telescopic drawer runners
  • False ceiling framing, plasterboard, and electrical wire running
  • Wall putty application, sanding, primer, and double-coat painting
  • Site debris clearance and deep post-construction floor scrubbing
  • 50-point snag checklist and manufacturer warranty handoff
Typically Excluded (Client Scope / Optional):
  • Loose consumer electronics (TVs, Refrigerator, Microwave, Washing Machine)
  • Loose soft furnishings (Curtains, Mattresses, Bed linens, Rugs)
  • Society meter shifting or society development security deposits
  • Major structural civil demolitions requiring society engineer NOC
  • Gas pipeline shifting (must be done by Adani/Gujarat Gas)
Smart Comparison

How to Compare Two Interior Quotations in Ahmedabad

If Contractor A quotes ₹8 Lakh and Contractor B quotes ₹10 Lakh for the same 3BHK flat, Contractor A is not necessarily cheaper. Ask these 4 critical questions:

  1. What is the core board material? Is it genuine IS:303/IS:710 plywood or low-density particle board? Cheap quotes often substitute uncalibrated low-density board that sags within 18 months.
  2. What is the laminate thickness? Are shutters lined with 1mm laminate or thin 0.6mm/0.7mm liners that bubble in heat?
  3. What hardware brand is specified? Are drawer runners branded Hafele, Hettich, or Ebco, or unbranded local channels?
  4. Is false ceiling framing included? Does the quote include Saint-Gobain GI channels and electrical wires, or only the gypsum board?
