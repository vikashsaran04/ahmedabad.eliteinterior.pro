Integrated Client Care

How Vikash and Ramesh Guide Your Home

Every home completed by Elite Interior benefits directly from the active collaboration of both founders.

Phase 1: Planning & Commercial Clarity

Vikash conducts the initial requirement discovery, reviews architectural floor plans, establishes budget boundaries, and ensures you receive a clear, uninflated, itemized specification.

Phase 2: Technical Vetting & Site Prep

Ramesh conducts laser site measurements, checks electrical chases, inspects wall plumb lines, and aligns factory modular fabrication drawings to eliminate dimensional errors.

Phase 3: Execution & Coordinated Handover

Ramesh supervises on-site carpentry, stone fitting, and painting crews daily while Vikash maintains weekly progress updates, timeline monitoring, and transparent milestone tracking.
