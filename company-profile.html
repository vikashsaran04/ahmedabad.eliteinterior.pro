Our Company Story

Why Elite Interior Exists: Bridging Design & Execution

A grounded look at the problems homeowners face when coordinating multiple contractors in Ahmedabad, and how unified turnkey interior management replaces stress with predictability.

The Real Problem

The Fragmented Reality of Residential Interiors

Taking possession of a new 2BHK, 3BHK, or bungalow in Ahmedabad is one of life's most exciting milestones. Yet, within days of starting interior work, most homeowners encounter an overwhelming web of conflicting trades.

Typically, a homeowner hires an independent designer who prepares appealing 3D drawings. Next, they attempt to engage separate carpenters, an electrician, a false ceiling team, a civil contractor, and painters. The moment execution begins, friction starts:

  • The carpenter blames the electrician for running conduits behind modular wardrobe carcases, causing misaligned back-panels.
  • The painter blames the false ceiling contractor for uneven plaster joints that create visible shadows under 3000K cove lights.
  • The 3D design proves structurally impossible because the designer failed to account for structural RCC beam drops, water pipes, or minimum hinge clearances.
  • Estimates spiral by 30% to 50% due to hidden "item exclusions" and verbal promises that were never written into the contract.

Elite Interior was built to eliminate this friction entirely. We believe that design, material procurement, carpentry, services coordination, and site supervision must operate as one accountable, synchronized discipline.

Integrated Methodology

Why Design, Budgeting, and Craftsmanship Cannot Be Separated

In many design firms, the person drafting the drawings has never spent a full day on an active Ahmedabad construction site. They don't know the exact kerf loss when cutting an 8x4 ply sheet, or how Gujarat's dry summer heat affects water-based adhesives on acrylic surfaces.

At Elite Interior, co-founders Vikash Kumar (Brand, Systems & Client Communication) and Ramesh Taak (Hands-on Carpentry, Modular Fabrication & Site Execution) bring complementary perspectives to every home:

The Rule of Constructability

Before any 3D render is presented to a client for final approval, it is vetted for site feasibility, structural durability, and exact cost impact. If a design concept requires non-standard hardware that lacks local replacement parts in Ahmedabad, we advise the homeowner honestly upfront.

This ensures that what you approve on screen is exactly what gets built on your site—within the committed budget, without unpleasant surprise variations halfway through the project.

Practical Living

Space Planning for Ahmedabad Lifestyles: Practicality First

Modern residential apartments across South Bopal, Shela, Gota, and Vaishnodevi Circle offer thoughtful layouts, but every square inch of carpet area needs intelligent planning.

We design around real household habits:

Daily Kitchen Ergonomics

Planning for Indian cooking spices, heavy stainless steel pressure cookers, mixer grinder pullouts, and heavy-duty tandem drawer runners with 35kg+ weight ratings.

Dust-Proof Wardrobe Seals

Ahmedabad's arid climate brings fine dust. We incorporate brush seals, concealed soft-close hinges, and calibrated loft heights to keep clothing pristine.

Communication Standard

Transparent Milestones & Scheduled Updates

Homeowners frequently tell us their biggest fear is "contractor silence"—when work stalls and nobody answers calls.

Under our operational workflow led by Vikash Kumar, every project follows a defined communication protocol:

  • Dedicated WhatsApp Coordination Group: Directly connects the homeowner with both co-founders and site supervisors.
  • Weekly Progress Photo Logs: Highlighting framing, electrical conduit laying, modular factory progress, and on-site assembly.
  • Milestone-Linked Payments: You pay in structured phases linked to visible, verified deliverables—never bulk advances upfront.
Craftsmanship

The 50-Point Snag Audit & Handover Protocol

We do not consider a project complete when the carpenters pack their tools. Handover is an intentional process:

Before handover, Ramesh Taak personally conducts a comprehensive quality audit:

  1. Checking smooth glide and alignment of all drawers and wardrobe sliding channels.
  2. Inspecting 0.8mm and 1mm laminate edges for flush trimming and absence of sharp seams.
  3. Testing all electrical switch plates, LED strip drivers, and two-way bedside controls.
  4. Reviewing paint surface uniformity under natural and artificial lighting.
  5. Complete debris clearance and deep post-construction floor scrubbing.
