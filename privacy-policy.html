Privacy Policy

How Elite Interior respects and protects the personal information you share with us.

1. Commitment to Privacy

Elite Interior ("we", "our", or "us"), operating at A-212a, Sun South Trade, South Bopal, Ahmedabad, respects the privacy of prospective clients, homeowners, and website visitors.

This website (ahmedabad.eliteinterior.pro) serves as our company authority and informational resource portal. We are committed to transparency in how any data you submit is handled.

2. Static Architecture & Zero Tracking Cookies

This subdomain is constructed using clean, static HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. We do not use intrusive third-party behavioral ad trackers or cross-site fingerprinting scripts.
